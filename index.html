GOOD TO KNOW

Common questions.

Can windows be made to custom dimensions?

Window dimensions and configurations can be planned for your project. Contact the team with your measurements to discuss the available options.

What is the difference between PVC and aluminium?

PVC and aluminium use different frame materials and have different design characteristics. The best option depends on the opening, appearance and project requirements.

Can I request installation?

Use the contact details below to ask about measurement, delivery and installation for your location.
